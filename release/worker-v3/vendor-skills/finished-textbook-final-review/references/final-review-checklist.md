# Final Textbook Review Checklist

Use this checklist for delivery review of finished HTML/PDF教材.

## Front Matter

- Cover title, grade, edition, term, brand, and subject match file name and body.
- Course description reflects actual chapters and does not invent absent lessons.
- TOC entries match rendered body page starts.
- No internal notes, debug text, file paths, source-cleaning comments, or uncertainty notes appear.

## Body

- Every unit has complete headings, explanations, examples, practice, answer space, and figures where expected.
- Every unit's substantive final paragraph/task appears in the final PDF; a heading-only or partially flushed final unit is a hard blocker.
- Questions preserve stems, subparts, options, answer blanks, and answer keys.
- Every visible written-response prompt has adequate answer room before the next task or chapter boundary.
- Concepts, examples, and practice align.

## Layout

- No clipped tables, broken images, raw MathJax/LaTeX, overflow, orphan headings, or unexpected blank pages.
- No chapter heading overlaps the prior body line or begins in the bottom 42 percent of a page.
- No visible Markdown image syntax/editorial alt text remains, and low-resolution photos are not enlarged into dominant print areas.
- No content-bearing figure is followed by duplicated OCR labels; a figure plus ruled answer lines is valid structured content rather than a low-text orphan.
- SVG/chart/geometry labels align with their semantic anchors.
- The all-page render ledger hash and page count match the exact final PDF being accepted.

## Editions

- Student edition hides/removes answers and teacher-only support as intended.
- Teacher edition retains answers/explanations.
- Page count and TOC differences are intentional and documented.
